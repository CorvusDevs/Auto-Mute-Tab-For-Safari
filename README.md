A native **Safari Web Extension** for **iPhone, iPad, and Mac**. The moment you switch tabs or windows, every background tab is muted, and audio restores the instant you switch back. It silences sites that bypass the normal mute button through the Web Audio API (YouTube, Twitch, X), keeps Picture-in-Picture videos playing, and lets you pin individual tabs. Zero accounts, zero telemetry, zero tracking.

## Features

- **Smart auto-muting.** Background tabs are silenced the moment you switch away, and audio restores seamlessly when you return. No configuration needed.
- **Per-tab controls.** Keep a specific tab playing in the background, mute the active tab on demand, or always allow a site, all from the popup. A dedicated page lists every tab currently muted with one-click unmute.
- **Picture-in-Picture aware.** Videos in PiP keep their audio even when their tab is in the background, so you never lose the sound on a floating video.
- **Cross-window muting.** Audio follows your focus across every Safari window, not just within one.
- **Customizable keyboard shortcut.** Toggle muting on or off instantly with a hotkey you set yourself.
- **Always-allow list.** Exclude music players, video calls, or any site that should always play audio.
- **Deep media support.** Handles HTML5 video and audio, autoplay, iframes, and sites that route sound through the Web Audio API where the normal mute button does nothing.
- **Recovers from sleep.** Audio comes back correctly after your Mac or device wakes, a case many tab muters miss.
- **33 languages.** Fully localized including the settings UI and onboarding, with right-to-left layout for Arabic and Hebrew.

## What makes Auto Mute Tab special

🔇 **It silences what Safari's own mute can't.** Sites like YouTube, Twitch, and X route audio through the Web Audio API, which slips past the normal per-tab mute. Auto Mute Tab mutes at the tab level so those sites actually go quiet.

🎯 **Truly automatic.** No buttons to remember. Switch tabs and the rest go silent; switch back and your audio returns. The one tab you're looking at is the only one you hear.

🪟 **Follows you across windows.** Most tab muters only think about one window. Auto Mute Tab moves the sound to whichever Safari window you focus.

🎛️ **Per-tab control when you want it.** Pin a background tab to keep playing, force-mute the active one, or mark a site as always allowed. See everything that's muted at a glance.

🌐 **33 languages, fully translated.** Including the in-app settings, the onboarding, and this site. RTL layout for Arabic and Hebrew. The language picker is one click away.

🛡️ **Built for privacy.** No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, no network requests. Everything runs locally in Safari. Your browsing stays completely private.

## Built with

**Safari Web Extension MV3** · **WebKit** · **SwiftUI**

## Privacy

No data collection, no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking. Auto Mute Tab never phones home and makes no network requests. All settings and your allow list live in `browser.storage.local` on your device.
